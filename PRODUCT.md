# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Dual audience: casual football fans checking World Cup 2026 match results, standings, and schedules during the tournament; and betting-focused users monitoring Asian handicap odds alongside live scores. Both groups access the scoreboard on desktop and mobile, often during or immediately after matches, valuing quick glanceability and real-time accuracy.

## Product Purpose

A real-time World Cup 2026 scoreboard that displays group standings, live match scores, bracket progression, and Asian handicap odds in one unified view. Success means a fan can open the page and instantly understand the tournament state — who's playing, who's winning, what the odds are — without clicking through multiple pages.

## Brand Personality

Dark, Intense, Sporty. The interface should feel like a stadium scoreboard at night: high-contrast, glowing accents, confident typography. Energy comes from vibrant blues and purples against a deep dark background. No gradients, no glassmorphism — solid colors, sharp edges, purposeful glow on live indicators.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards with cream/sand backgrounds and muted tones
- Overdesigned "modern" interfaces with glassmorphism, gradient text, or decorative blurs
- Cluttered sports sites with excessive ads and dense information
- AI-generated landing page patterns (numbered sections, side-stripe borders, tiny uppercase kickers)

## Design Principles

1. **Data first, chrome second** — Every element earns its place by conveying match information. Decoration that doesn't inform gets cut.
2. **Glanceable hierarchy** — Scores and status must be readable instantly. Group info, bracket positions, and odds follow in decreasing urgency.
3. **Dark intensity** — Deep backgrounds with purposeful highlights. Glow is reserved for live matches and critical updates only.
4. **Consistent density** — The same spacing scale, type ramp, and color roles apply to groups, bracket, and odds. No orphaned styles.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA minimum (4.5:1 body text contrast)
- Live score updates respect prefers-reduced-motion
- Tabular data is screen-reader friendly with proper scope attributes
- Color is never the sole differentiator for match status
